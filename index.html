How to play

  • Bowl: put your finger on the ball and swipe up toward the pins. Swipe faster to bowl harder.
  • Move the ball: slide your finger sideways first, then swipe up.
  • Curve it: bend your swipe and the ball curves the same way into the pins.
  • Strike: all ten pins with your first ball. Fireworks! You score 10 plus your next two balls.
  • Spare: all the pins with two balls. You score 10 plus your next ball.
  • Power-ups: knock down every pin to win one. Tap it before you bowl to use it: 🚀 Rainbow Boost, 🎈 Giant Ball, 🍀 Lucky Bounce, ✨ Sparkle Burst.
  • Bumpers stop the ball falling in the gutter. Turn them on in the menu.
  • Ten frames make a game. The tenth frame gives bonus balls for a strike or spare. 300 is a perfect game!
