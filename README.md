# 🌈 Rainbow Bowling

Ten-pin bowling with a rainbow ball. It leaves a rainbow trail down the lane,
the pins sparkle, a strike sets off fireworks and a spare always makes a silly
noise. Play on your own or pass the phone for up to four players.

**Play it:** https://rvenning.github.io/rainbow-bowling/

Designed from a brief written with an eight-year-old: a rainbow ball, a
rainbow trail, sparkly pins, power-ups for knocking everything down,
fireworks for a strike and something silly for a spare.

## Features

- **Swipe to bowl.** Flick the ball up the lane: the direction is where it
  goes and a faster flick bowls harder, in four broad steps, so even a lazy
  push reaches the pins. A gentle aim assist pulls near misses into the
  pocket. Drag sideways first to move the ball across the foul line. Bend the
  swipe and the ball hooks the same way, still arriving where it was aimed.
  A finger animation teaches the swipe on the first few throws.
- **Real ten-pin rules and scoring.** Strikes, spares, splits, the tenth
  frame's bonus balls, running totals that wait for their bonus balls, and a
  proper score sheet. 300 is a perfect game.
- **Real pin action.** The ball and pins are a small physics simulation on a
  lane of true dimensions. Falling pins are fifteen inches long and knock
  their neighbours over, pins bounce off the kickbacks, and a ball in the
  pocket carries the rack.
- **A camera that follows the ball** from behind the foul line down to the
  pins, so the pins are big and close when it arrives.
- **Power-ups.** Knock every pin down (a strike or a spare) to win one; hold
  up to three and tap one before you bowl:
  - 🚀 **Rainbow Boost**: a super-heavy, extra-fast ball.
  - 🎈 **Giant Ball**: the ball grows huge for one throw.
  - 🍀 **Lucky Bounce**: bumpers pop up and the ball curls toward the pins.
  - ✨ **Sparkle Burst**: the ball bursts with sparkles at the pins.
- **Celebrations.** Fireworks for a strike (a bigger show for a double and a
  turkey), and one of four silly sounds for a spare: slide whistle, boing,
  clown horn, kazoo.
- **Single Player** to beat your best, or **Pass and Play** for 2 to 4:
  family profiles, guests with a typed name, and **Robo-Rolly**, a computer
  player to bowl against. Everyone bowls a frame, then passes the phone. The
  game says whose turn it is, and ties are celebrated as ties.
- **Bumpers** per player, like a real alley's kids' lanes. Bumper games keep
  their own best score.
- **Family leaderboard**: best game, best with bumpers, and lifetime strikes.
- Synthesised sound (wooden pin clacks, a rolling rumble, a little disco
  loop), with separate sound and music switches, plus a speedy-sweeper option
  for shorter pauses. Respects reduced motion.

## Built on gamekit

Profiles, PINs, family sync, the leaderboard, sounds, PWA install and the
version line come from [gamekit](https://github.com/rvenning/gamekit),
vendored into `lib/`. To update it:

```
node ../gamekit/tools/sync-to-game.js .
node lib/tools/stamp-version.js . --bump
```

## How it is put together

- `js/scoring.js`: ten-pin scoring from a list of pins per ball. Frames,
  marks, totals and "where does the next ball come from" are all derived.
- `js/lane.js`: the lane, in inches, top-down. Fixed 1/480 s step, circles
  plus a swinging capsule for each falling pin, no `Math.random`.
- `js/game.js`: `Match`, the turn order, rack, power-up trays and the pace
  between balls. DOM-free.
- `js/bot.js`: one bowler for Robo-Rolly, the title screen and the tests,
  with skill expressed as how far its aim and power wobble.
- `js/render.js`: the perspective alley, pins, rainbow ball and trail.
- `js/fireworks.js`, `js/input.js`, `js/audio.js`, `js/main.js`: the
  fireworks overlay, the swipe, sound, and the app shell.
- `js/swipe.js`: reads a finger track as a throw (direction, aim assist,
  speed band, bend). Pure, so `tests/swipe.test.js` feeds it made-up swipes.
- `tools/sweep.js`: balance probe. What a throw knocks down, and how each
  bot skill scores over many games.

## PWA

`manifest.json`, `sw.js` (network-first, cache fallback) and `icons/`
(generated by `node tools/make-icons.js`). Bump the version with
`node lib/tools/stamp-version.js . --bump` before every deploy.

## Local development

```
npx http-server . -p 8144 -c-1
```

Then open http://localhost:8144/. Add `?debug=1` for dev tools (saving is
switched off in debug mode).

## Tests

```
npm test
```

Checks the scoring rules (perfect game, all spares, the tenth frame, totals
that never go down), that the lane is deterministic and every throw finishes,
that bumpers and each power-up do what they say, turn order for 1 to 4
players, the save merge both ways round, and that bots from a six-year-old to
a sharp adult all finish every game with scores in skill order.

## Storage

localStorage keys are prefixed `rbowl_`; family sync uses the `rainbowbowling`
Firestore collection in the shared family-games Firebase project. The API key
in `js/firebase-config.js` is a public client key restricted to Firestore, and
the rules require an anonymous sign-in.
